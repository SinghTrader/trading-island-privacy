Trading Island Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Trading Island is a Chrome extension designed to provide financial market information, personal watchlists, and trading profit and loss tracking.

Data Stored Locally

Trading Island stores information such as watchlist selections, P&L calendar entries, interface position, size, and preferences locally in the user's browser using Chrome storage.

Market Data Requests

When Trading Island retrieves market information, the requested financial instrument symbol is sent to the Trading Island backend service hosted using Supabase. The backend may communicate with third-party market-data providers to retrieve the requested market information.

Infrastructure providers may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, and protect the service, such as request metadata.

P&L Information

Profit and loss entries entered into the Trading Island calendar are stored locally in the user's browser and are not intentionally transmitted to the Trading Island market-data backend.

Sale of Personal Data

Trading Island does not sell users' personal information.

Financial Disclaimer

Trading Island provides market information for informational purposes only. Nothing provided by Trading Island constitutes financial or investment advice.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Trading Island develops and introduces new features. Updates will be published on this page.
